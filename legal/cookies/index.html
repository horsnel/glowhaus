Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 2026

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us recognize your browser, remember your preferences, and keep you signed in across sessions. GlowHaus uses cookies and similar technologies (local storage, session storage) to operate the service, secure your account, and understand how the product is used.

We do not sell cookie-based data to third parties, and we never use cookies to build advertising profiles of you across other sites. The cookies we set are limited to what is strictly necessary to run the product, plus a small set of analytics cookies that help us improve.

Cookies We Use

We group the cookies on glowhaus.app into four categories:

  • Strictly Necessary — Authentication session tokens, CSRF tokens, and load-balancer cookies. These are required for the site to function and cannot be disabled.
  • Preference — Remembered theme, language, and onboarding state. These persist across sessions and improve your experience.
  • Analytics — Aggregated, anonymized usage metrics (page views, feature clicks, error rates). We use these to find bugs and prioritize improvements.
  • Functional — Recently viewed styles, upload drafts, and "continue where you left off" state. These power product features you have explicitly used.

We do not use marketing, advertising, or cross-site tracking cookies.

Specific Cookies

The table below lists the cookies glowhaus.app may set on your device:

  • gh_session — Authentication session. Expires after 30 days of inactivity. Strictly necessary.
  • gh_prefs — UI preferences (theme, language). Persists for 1 year. Preference.
  • gh_draft — Upload draft state. Persists for 7 days. Functional.
  • gh_anon_id — Anonymous analytics identifier. Persists for 13 months. Analytics.
  • __cf_bm — Cloudflare bot management. Expires after 30 minutes. Strictly necessary.
  • __stripe_mid / __stripe_sid — Fraud prevention (when paying). 1 year / 30 minutes. Strictly necessary.

Third-Party Services

Some third-party services we rely on may set their own cookies when you interact with them:

  • Supabase — Authentication provider. Sets session cookies when you sign in.
  • Paystack — Payment processor. Sets fraud-prevention cookies on checkout.
  • Cloudflare — CDN and security. Sets the __cf_bm bot-management cookie.
  • Google OAuth — Optional sign-in. Sets cookies on the Google sign-in flow only.

Each of these services has its own cookie policy. We recommend reviewing them if you have concerns.

Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Most browsers let you:

  • Block all cookies (will break sign-in and uploads).
  • Block only third-party cookies.
  • Clear cookies when you close the browser.
  • Review and delete individual cookies.

Blocking strictly-necessary cookies will prevent you from signing in, uploading photos, or generating looks. Blocking analytics cookies will not affect product functionality.

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change. Significant changes (such as adding a new category of cookies) will be announced via email and an in-app notification at least 7 days before they take effect.

Continued use of GlowHaus after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
